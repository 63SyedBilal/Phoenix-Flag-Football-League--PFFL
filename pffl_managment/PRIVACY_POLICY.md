# Privacy Policy for Phoenix Flag Football League (PFFL) Management App

**Effective Date:** December 30, 2025

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to Phoenix Flag Football League (PFFL) Management App ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Personal Information
- Name, email address, phone number
- Profile pictures and team photos
- Payment information (processed securely through Stripe)
- Device information and location data

### 2.2 Usage Data
- App usage statistics
- Device information (model, OS version)
- Error logs and crash reports
- Performance metrics

### 2.3 Payment Information
- Payment details are processed securely through Stripe
- We do not store credit card information on our servers

## 3. How We Use Your Information

- **Account Management:** Create and manage your user account
- **App Functionality:** Provide league management features
- **Payment Processing:** Process league registration fees
- **Communication:** Send notifications about games and league updates
- **App Improvement:** Analyze usage and fix technical issues
- **Customer Support:** Respond to your inquiries and support requests

## 4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties except in the following circumstances:

- **Service Providers:** Stripe for payment processing
- **Legal Requirements:** When required by law or to protect our rights
- **Business Transfers:** In case of merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

## 5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information:

- SSL/TLS encryption for data transmission
- Secure server infrastructure
- Regular security audits
- Access controls and authentication

## 6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. Payment information is not stored on our servers.

## 7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

- Access your personal information
- Correct inaccurate information
- Delete your account and data
- Opt out of marketing communications
- Data portability

## 8. Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy in the app.

## 11. Consent

By using our app, you consent to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.
